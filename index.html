Our approach

Explore. Experiment. Evolve.

Good products rarely appear fully formed. We prefer a thoughtful process that keeps the problem visible and makes room to learn along the way.

01

Explore the problem

Start with the people, the context and the friction. Get clear about what needs to improve before deciding what to build.

02

Experiment with ideas

Make a small, testable version. Learn from real use, ask better questions and keep what genuinely helps.

03

Evolve what works

Improve the experience with feedback, build carefully and grow the product at a pace that makes sense.

A few quick answers

Curious about the lab?

We're at the beginning of the journey, and we'd rather be clear about that than make promises before the work is done.

What is Tech Mak Labs?

Tech Mak Labs is an independent digital product studio exploring ideas across AI, apps, games and marketing technology. The goal is to build useful digital experiences that solve specific problems.

Can I use your products today?

Some ideas are still being explored. Tech Mak Search is an early product direction, not a finished live service. We'll update this website as projects become ready to share.

Do you work with businesses or individuals?

We're interested in problems from both. If you have an idea for a useful tool, an everyday frustration or a challenge faced by a small business, we'd like to hear about it.

Can I suggest a product idea?

Absolutely. Use the idea form above to choose an area and write a short note. Your email app will open with a draft that you can review and send.

Is Tech Mak Labs focused only on AI?

No. AI is one area we explore, alongside apps, games, practical utilities and marketing technology. The problem should determine the technology, not the other way around.
