How to interpret the results

  • A general Queries CSV and a general Pages CSV do not reveal which URL ranked for each individual query. Filtered Pages exports provide that relationship.
  • “Not observed” means absent from the uploaded export; it does not mean zero search demand.
  • This version does not fetch live rankings, competitor data, map-pack results or keyword volumes.
  • Do not redirect or delete a page based only on a possible overlap flag. Review search intent, URL history and page performance first.
